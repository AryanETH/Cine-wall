# Share CineWall

Give each post one useful demo or lesson. Pick a community whose rules allow project demos, introduce yourself as the builder, and ask for feedback people can give from their own setup.

| When | What to share | Where it fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | A short clip of one movie across two laptops, plus the three setup steps | DIY home theatre and movie night communities |
| Week 2 | A quick comparison of normal sound and 3D Sound on the same track | Audio, music, and browser app communities |
| Week 3 | A tutorial for mirroring a slide deck or demo to spare laptops | Teaching, presentation, and maker communities |
| Week 4 | A short build note on keeping multiple browser players in sync | Web development and self-hosting communities |

Use the [live demo](https://watch.aitoyz.in/#demo) or [setup tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nKjiv6_XSN4). Keep an eye on which demos lead to real feedback, then use that feedback to choose the next post.

### First post draft

I wanted a wider screen for movie night, so I built CineWall. It plays one video across two or three laptops and keeps the controls in one browser dashboard. The bezels are visible, but the setup uses the laptops you already have.

Here’s the demo: https://watch.aitoyz.in/#demo

If you try it, I’d love to know what laptops and browsers you used and whether the screens stayed in sync.
