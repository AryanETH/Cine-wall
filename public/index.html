ABOUT CINEWALL

Built for laptops.
Made for sharing.

CineWall is a free home cinema and watch-party browser app built by Aryan. Watch together on a laptop video wall, fill the room with 3D audio playlists, or share a live screen with friends. It also includes room calls and a phone microphone with a teleprompter and voice recording.

No special display hardware is needed for a video wall. Screen edges stay visible, and playback depends on your Wi-Fi and each laptop's browser.

Devices & browsers

Which devices can I use with CineWall?

Video streaming, music streaming and screen sharing are designed for laptops and desktop PCs. Start with a current Chrome or Edge browser. On smartphones, Mic and Speaker is available in portrait mode; the other modes show a laptop-only warning.

Can I use my phone as a microphone or speaker?

Yes. Open Mic and Speaker on your phone in portrait mode. Choose Phone mic to broadcast your voice, or open a numbered speaker link to listen. A typical room uses one phone microphone and up to three laptop speakers. Phone microphone access needs trusted HTTPS and your permission.

Do I need an app, extension or special display?

No app or browser extension is needed to use the online website. Open CineWall in your browser. A video wall uses the laptops you already have, placed side by side; their screen edges stay visible. The optional local server is a separate setup for use on your own network.

Getting started

Is CineWall free?

Yes. The video wall, 3D audio playlists, screen sharing and Mic and Speaker features are free to use. Supporting the project is optional.

How do I set up a mini theatre at home?

Connect your laptops to the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. Choose Stream a video on one laptop, select your video file, and open a different numbered screen link on each laptop. Place the screens side by side, wait until they are ready, and press Play on the source dashboard.

Can I watch Netflix or other subscription services here?

CineWall plays your selected video or audio files, and the Video and Audio dashboards include YouTube downloads. It does not integrate Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video or other subscription services. Protected video may appear black when screen sharing.

Which video and music files work?

MP4 with H.264 video and AAC sound is the safest video choice. Some WebM and H.264/AAC MKV files also work. For music, try MP3, WAV or AAC. Each laptop must support the file's codecs. CineWall checks your selection and does not convert it.

Hosting & joining

How do I invite friends to my room?

Choose a mode on the source laptop and copy its numbered screen or speaker links to the other laptops. Each link includes the room ID. Opening the homepage separately can create a different room, even on the same Wi-Fi. Keep the source dashboard open while you play.

Does everyone need their own copy of the file?

No. Choose your movie or songs on the source laptop only. It shares the selected file with the devices that join its room.

How many screens or speakers can join?

Video walls support 1–3 laptops, with 2–3 recommended for a wider picture. Audio rooms support up to 5 speakers and 10 songs. Screen sharing supports up to 10 numbered screens, plus the presenter. An optional room call supports up to 4 devices. Mic and Speaker supports one phone and up to three speaker slots.

Can we talk while sharing a screen?

Yes. Join the room call from the Screen sharing page, then enable your microphone or camera if you want. Up to four devices can join the call. Use headphones to reduce echo. Camera and microphone access need a secure connection and browser permission.

Can I present slides or a document?

Open the file in its usual app, choose Screen sharing on a laptop or PC, and share that window, tab or screen. Viewers see the same view. This does not create an extended desktop or let one computer control another computer's apps.

Playback & sound

Do songs play automatically in order?

Yes. Add up to ten songs and drag them into order. When a song ends, the next one loads and starts after every selected speaker is ready. If there is no next song, the current song plays again. Keep the source dashboard open.

How do I try 3D sound?

Click Try in 3D in the audio player or select 3D Sound in Settings. The recording's left and right channels are spread across laptops in speaker-number order. A smooth gradient follows the stereo mix and grows where sound is louder. Volume, mute and pause affect it too. Mono recordings stay balanced; CineWall does not create Dolby Atmos.

Why do I need to enable sound on each laptop?

Browsers can block sound until you interact with a page. Click Enable sound or Enable speaker once when asked, then use the source dashboard for playback and individual volume controls.

Why can playback or sound have a little delay?

Wi-Fi, browser buffering and device speakers can add different delays. Use the same strong Wi-Fi, keep the source laptop awake and avoid Bluetooth speakers for less delay. Screen sharing Auto quality reduces traffic as more viewers join. Exact timing across every device is not guaranteed.

Privacy & troubleshooting

What is the difference between Instant and Upload?

Instant shares a file from the source laptop; keep its dashboard open. On the online website, use the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. Upload first saves a temporary copy on the server for the other screens to play. Neither option converts the file.

Who can join my session?

Share your room and numbered device links only with people you want to join. Those links identify your room, so treat them as private. Your browser asks for permission before using your microphone, camera or screen.

Can CineWall work without internet?

The online website needs internet to load and connect your room. For local use without internet, run the CineWall server on one laptop and connect the others to the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. Phone microphone access still needs trusted HTTPS; see Mic and Speaker setup help.

What should I check if a screen will not play?

Open the correct numbered link, check that every laptop is on the same Wi-Fi, and keep the source dashboard awake. Enable sound when asked and wait for each screen to report ready. If Instant cannot connect, allow local network access if prompted or try Upload. Refresh pages after a website update so every device uses the same version.
