A SIMPLE SETUP

One file. A bigger view.

Choose once on one laptop. The other screens follow.

  1. 01

    Get together

    Connect your laptops to the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. Place them side by side for a movie wall.

  2. 02

    Choose and connect

    Choose Video or Audio, then select your file. Open its numbered screen links on the other laptops.

  3. 03

    Press play

    Wait for the screens to be ready. Play, pause and change the sound from the laptop that chose the file.

ABOUT CINEWALL

Built for laptops.
Made for sharing.

CineWall is an open-source browser app built by Aryan. It brings devices you already own together for movies, music and live screen sharing.

No special display hardware is needed for a movie wall. Screen edges stay visible, and playback depends on your Wi-Fi and each laptop’s browser.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions, answered.

If I can watch on one screen, why use other screens?

You don’t have to. Use CineWall when you want a wider picture made from two or three laptops you already own, or music from several laptops. Each movie screen shows a different part of the same frame. The screen edges and extra setup are the trade-off.

Does every laptop need the movie or song?

No. Choose the file on one laptop only. That laptop becomes the admin and shares it with the screens that join its room.

Why do laptops on the same Wi-Fi get different rooms?

Wi-Fi connects the devices, but the website needs a room link to put them together. Open the admin’s numbered screen links instead of opening the homepage separately. Those links contain the same room ID.

What is the difference between Instant and Server?

Instant shares the file from the admin laptop and keeps that tab open. On the online site, the laptops need the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. Server uploads a copy first, then the screens play from it. Neither mode converts your movie.

Can I use CineWall without internet?

Yes, with the local CineWall server running on one laptop and all devices on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. The online website still needs internet to load pages and connect the room.

Which files work?

MP4 with H.264 video and AAC sound is the safest video choice. Some WebM and H.264/AAC MKV files also work. For music, try MP3, WAV or AAC. Every laptop must be able to play the file. CineWall checks your selection and does not convert it.

Why might I need to tap each laptop for sound?

Browsers can block sound until you interact with that page. Tap Enable sound once when asked, then use the admin’s playback controls.

Is 3D mode surround sound?

It spreads the recording’s left and right sound across your laptops, in screen-number order. Movement comes from the song or movie’s stereo mix. It does not create Dolby Atmos, and a mono track stays mono.

Can I use a laptop as a second desktop monitor?

Screen sharing shows a view you choose: a tab, a window or your current desktop. It does not create extra desktop space. A true extended desktop needs a desktop app and a virtual display or display adapter.

Can one mouse and keyboard control all the computers?

CineWall can show a shared pointer inside the shared view. Controlling other computers’ apps or desktops needs installed software such as Deskflow. A website cannot move another computer’s system cursor or type into its other apps.

What is available today?

Video, Audio and live Screen sharing are available. Presentation and live YouTube modes are still being worked on; their cards are marked Coming soon.
