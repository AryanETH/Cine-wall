From the Community

A few quick answers.

Is CineWall free?

Yes. All four tools are free. No app or extension needed.

Which devices work?

Use laptops or PCs for video, audio and screen sharing. A portrait phone can be your microphone.

How do I connect my laptops?

Use the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. Open each numbered device link and keep the source dashboard open.

Which files can I play?

MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio works best. Other formats depend on your browser’s codecs.

Will the screens be seamless?

Laptop edges stay visible. Playback timing depends on your network, browsers and devices.
